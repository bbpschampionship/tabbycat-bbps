Vox Debating

Championship

The intra-school debating championship of Shalom Presidency School, Gurugram — run in the World Schools (WSDC) format, in collaboration with Taivas.

How the tournament runs
Road to the Grand Final
1
Opening Round — all teams debate one motion; results seed the knockout draw.
2
Pre-Quarter-finals — the top 16 teams by cumulative team & speaker score advance.
3
Quarter-finals & Semi-finals — the field narrows to the final two.
4
Grand Final — the top two teams debate before the full school audience.
