How the qualifier works

AttendanceYour batch's judge marks everyone present at the start of each round.
Three rounds of 1v1 debate — all compulsoryEvery student must debate in all three rounds. The judge announces the pairs and each pair's motion. Round 1 is random; Rounds 2 and 3 pair speakers on similar scores.
Scored out of 100Feedback is given verbally. Each speaker gets one score out of 100, and the winner of every debate always scores higher.
Top {{ release.qualify_count|default:50 }} in each track qualifyRankings by total score across the three rounds decide who goes through to the in-person outrounds. Results are announced at 6:00 PM.
